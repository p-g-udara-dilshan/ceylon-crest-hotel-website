HELPFUL INFORMATION

Before you go.

Answers to the questions we hear most often.

What time is check-in and check-out?+

Our suggested check-in is from 2:00 pm and check-out by 11:00 am. Please check with the hotel for final timings before travelling.

Can I request a particular room?+

Of course. Select your preferred room when preparing an enquiry. Specific room availability and requests need to be confirmed by the hotel team.

Are children welcome?+

Yes, our Family Suite is designed with extra space for up to four guests. For larger groups, please send an enquiry.

Is my reservation confirmed after submitting?+

No. This website prepares a booking enquiry and estimated stay cost. An actual reservation requires confirmation directly from the hotel.
